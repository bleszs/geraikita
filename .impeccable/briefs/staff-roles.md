# Akun pemilik, kasir dan dapur

Mode: Operate. Ordinary extension of Gerai Kita; preserve DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json.

Users operate a small food business in Indonesian on a laptop or phone. Owner manages staff in Pengaturan akun; cashier enters real orders and hands over ready orders; kitchen sees tasks and preparation notes, operates cooking stages and optional existing voice controls. Keep the established cream/terracotta, Nunito Sans, tokens, buttons, forms and dark theme.

First viewport: business name, personal role/name, connection state and active shared queue. Cashier additionally sees intake, menu prices and availability. Kitchen receives no owner finances or ingredients. No dashboard metrics or decorative illustration. The signature interaction is a shared order advancing from cashier intake through kitchen stages to cashier handover, with keyboard focus restored after each update.

Owner must explicitly confirm account creation or changes; show initial password handling, affected sessions and active/inactive status. Staff have their own next/skip tour and password form. Mobile wraps header controls and uses a single task column. Offline disables mutation; no persistent staff snapshot, private owner drafts or owner navigation. Avoid claims of production deployment or two-second speech.

Code-led, no new visual world or approved comp. Required captures: cashier desktop/mobile, kitchen desktop/mobile dark, owner settings desktop/mobile. Synthetic test fixtures only; never add staff or orders to real accounts automatically.
